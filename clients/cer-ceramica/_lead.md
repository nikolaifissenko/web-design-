- **Notes:**
  - **Everything on this demo is real, zero stock.** The shop's own
    WordPress.com blog, despite having zero custom design, turned out to
    hold a trove of real photos it uploaded itself over the years
    (2012-2015), several with verifiable EXIF metadata confirming real
    iPhone 4 camera shots, not stock: `manufacturer=Apple, model=iPhone
    4`. One image in particular, a promotional collage the shop made for
    itself ("alla-cer-tutto-si-puo.jpg"), contains both their real logo
    and a dozen small real class/workshop photos in one file.
  - **Story**: founded 1966, the oldest ceramics workshop and school in
    Rome, a two-minute walk from the Colosseum. Run by Annamaria
    Apolloni, who was also the subject of a 2012 documentary about
    ceramic art (per the blog's own post title, "Annamaria Apolloni
    protagonista di un documentario sulla ceramica"). Supplies both
    professional sculptors/ceramicists and hobbyists, and teaches a wide
    range of techniques, from wheel-throwing to raku and mosaic.
  - **Logo:** a real mark exists, visible in the shop's own promotional
    collage image: a maroon-bordered box with a stylized amphora icon
    above and below the text "C.E.R. / TUTTO PER LA CERAMICA". The full
    mark is text-heavy and rectangular, so it doesn't crop cleanly into
    the 40x40 circular slot. Per `branding-extraction.md`, redesigned a
    clean icon-only SVG (the amphora silhouette alone), colored with
    values sampled directly from the real logo image's own pixels, not
    invented: `#7a1618` (the maroon of the "C.E.R." text) and `#2c1810`
    (the near-black brown of the amphora graphic).
  - **Colors:** `#7a1618` and `#2c1810`, both pixel-sampled from the
    real logo image described above, not guessed. (The shop's WordPress
    theme itself uses generic default-theme colors like `#7a00df`
    purple, which were correctly ignored as theme noise, not brand
    colors.)
  - **Photos, all real, none stock**, all sourced from the shop's own
    WordPress uploads:
    - `hero.jpg`, a real class/workshop moment, pouring liquid clay
      slip into a plaster mold box (filename "imm-cer-stampi.jpg").
    - `about.jpg`, a real photo of a craftswoman shaping clay at a
      potter's wheel inside the actual workshop (visible real shelving
      in the background), cropped from the promotional collage image.
    - `gallery-1.jpg`, a real hand-built coil pot, cropped from the
      same collage.
    - `gallery-2.jpg`, real clay coils laid out for coil-building,
      cropped from the same collage.
  - Mood: **rustic** (warm, tactile, slightly imperfect), fits a
    hands-in-clay pottery workshop and school better than a more formal
    mood would.
