- **Notes:**
  - **Everything on this demo is real, zero stock.** Founded 1920 (date
    taken directly off the shop's own coffee packaging, see
    `gallery-1.jpg`, not from third-party sources that disagreed with
    each other on the year). Coffee is still roasted with an old
    wood-fired roaster, per the same 2016 blog feature.
  - **Photos, all real, sourced carefully:** the source blog
    (coffeeinrome.wixsite.com) reviews many different roasters, so every
    candidate image was downloaded and visually checked one by one
    before use, specifically to avoid the mistake of reusing a photo
    from a *different* roaster reviewed on the same blog (one rejected
    candidate was clearly a cup branded "Venchi", an unrelated chocolate
    chain, not Foroni). `hero.jpg`: the shop's real illuminated sign at
    night ("Torrefazione Drogheria"). `about.jpg`: the shop's real glass
    display case with a hand-written price tag reading "Caffè Foroni
    Miscela Tanzania €19.00/Kg", unambiguous proof it's their own
    counter. `gallery-1.jpg`: a macro shot of their real coffee
    packaging, showing the "Torrefazione Foroni dal 1920" mark and
    illustrated character. `gallery-2.jpg`: fresh grounds in a
    portafilter, from the same visit.
  - **Logo:** no vector logo exists online anywhere. A real mark does
    exist, photographed on their coffee packaging (a turbaned
    "moretto"-style coffee-house character in gold on dark brown foil,
    holding a small cup), but it's shot at an angle on reflective foil,
    not cleanly croppable. Per `branding-extraction.md`, redrew a clean
    icon in the same spirit and pose (`images/logo.svg`), colored with
    values sampled pixel-by-pixel from the real packaging photo (dark
    brown ~`#4a2c1e`, gold/bronze ~`#b8863f`), not invented.
  - Mood: **vintage** (Yeseva One display serif, warm sepia framing),
    matching the shop's own 1920s art-deco packaging design.
  - No prices published beyond the one hand-written tag visible in
    `about.jpg` (€19.00/Kg for the Tanzania blend), left blank elsewhere
    in `config.js` per the usual convention.
  - Hours (Mon-Sat 8:30-13:00 / 16:30-20:00, closed Sunday) confirmed
    via independent aggregator search, consistent across sources.
